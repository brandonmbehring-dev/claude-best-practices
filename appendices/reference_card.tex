\section{Settings Precedence}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Managed} (\code{.managed-settings.json}) --- IT-deployed, highest priority
  \item \textbf{Local} (\code{.claude/settings.local.json}) --- personal, auto-gitignored
  \item \textbf{Project} (\code{.claude/settings.json}) --- team standards, committed
  \item \textbf{User} (\code{\textasciitilde/.claude/settings.json}) --- global personal defaults
\end{enumerate}

Higher levels override lower. Within each level, \code{deny} rules are absolute
and cannot be overridden by lower levels.
